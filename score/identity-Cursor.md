# Kit identity

Container lengths were fixed before note generation. Every core part is 8 bars. Every arrangement is 32 bars. Meter is 4/4. No stochastic draws.

Patient downtempo development, evolving pitch fields, layered detail, and spacious interplay. The BPM range is a project choice, not a claim that these files are authentic genre productions.

Core versus reference: There is no reference-only part. All six roles are core. The arrangements reuse those files.

## k01 Low Water

Half-time pocket: kick on beat 1, snare on beat 3, hats only on the and of 2 and the last 16th. The bass leaps D to A, then settles. The motif rises D F A B and leaves bar 3 empty. Harmony holds Dm11 as D F C E G, then G9 as G B F A, then Cmaj7, then a thinner Dm. Atmosphere is a few long high notes with silence in the last bar.

Harmonic rhythm: Dm11 (D F C E G) for two bars, G9 (G B F A) for two, Cmaj7 for two, then a delayed Dm for two.

## k02 Glass Orbit

The kick pushes the and of 2 as well as beat 1. Claves sit on beats 2 and 4. Bass is a three-note pedal that changes root each bar and cadences on B. The motif descends in five short notes for four bars, then answers with fewer longer notes. Harmony staggers root, fifth, third, and seventh, one chord a bar. Atmosphere enters late in every other bar.

Harmonic rhythm: One chord per bar: Am, F, Cmaj7, Em7, Am, F, Dm, Am. Each tone enters a beat later than the last.

## k03 Dust Choir

Most of the bar is silent. Kick only on odd bars, hat on the even bars, snare on beat 4 of bars 4 and 8. Congas group attacks as 3+3+2 sixteenths twice in the bar. That grouping does not change the 4/4 meter. Bass is one long note per bar. The motif is eight calls spread over eight bars. Harmony is two held chords, F then Bb. Atmosphere is one note every four bars.

Harmonic rhythm: Fadd9 for four bars, then Bb for four bars.

Arrangement placements, in bars from 0: drums [24], percussion [24], bass [16, 24], motif [16, 24], harmonic-field [8, 16, 24], atmosphere [0, 8].

Limitations: Variation A and variation B are alternatives. Do not stack them. Do not mix parts from different kits: the tempos and collections differ. The 32-bar arrangement uses variation A only. Closed and open hats never overlap, and no hat-choke controller is written. Variation B of the motif uses E4 once, flagged as a departure, then returns to F.

## k04 Night Ferry

Faster tempo, still half-time in the kick: beats 1 and 3, with an 8th-note hat and an open hat on beat 4. Woodblocks answer just after each beat. Bass is four short syncopations, including the 9th F#. The motif is a downbeat arrival plus a pickup into the next bar. Harmony lands on beat 2 every two bars: Em9, G, D, Em. Atmosphere pulses a fifth once per bar.

Harmonic rhythm: Em9, G, Dsus, Em. Each chord starts on beat 2 and lasts two bars.

Arrangement placements, in bars from 0: drums [0, 8, 16], percussion [0, 16], bass [8, 16, 24], motif [16, 24], harmonic-field [8, 16], atmosphere [16, 24].

Limitations: Variation A and variation B are alternatives. Do not stack them. Do not mix parts from different kits: the tempos and collections differ. The 32-bar arrangement uses variation A only. Closed and open hats never overlap, and no hat-choke controller is written. Variation B of the bass uses C2 once, flagged as a departure, between E and D.
